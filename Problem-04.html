Weekend Destination Guide - Jaipur

Jaipur, the Pink City of Rajasthan, is famous for its forts, palaces, markets, food and colourful culture.

Attractions

Important attractions include Amber Palace, City Palace and Hawa Mahal.

Culture

Jaipur has a rich cultural heritage including traditional crafts, music, architecture and local markets.

Food

Popular Rajasthani dishes include dal baati churma, kachori and ghewar.

Best Time

Recommended season: October to March is generally comfortable for sightseeing.

Winter is a popular season for exploring Jaipur.

Travel Tips

Carry water, wear comfortable shoes, plan attractions beforehand and keep important documents safe.
