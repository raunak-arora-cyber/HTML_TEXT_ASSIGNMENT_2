Campus Innovation Week Brings Students Together

Students showcase technology projects

A week-long student innovation program brought together students from different academic backgrounds.

The event featured demonstrations related to programming, robotics, artificial intelligence and digital problem solving. Students presented their projects and explained their ideas.

Faculty members interacted with project teams and encouraged students to improve their prototypes through practical learning.

The main exhibition was held on 24 August 2026.

Students also participated in discussions about technology, innovation and future career opportunities.

The event highlighted teamwork, communication and practical learning.
