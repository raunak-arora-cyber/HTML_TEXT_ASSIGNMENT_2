About GLA University

GLA University is a private university located in Uttar Pradesh. The university provides education in engineering, management, pharmacy, law, education, science and several other disciplines.

Always check the official website for current university information.

Academic Programs

GLA University offers programs including Computer Science and Engineering, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, Electronics and Communication, Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering and other disciplines.

Technology education combines academic learning with practical projects and skill development.

Campus Life

The university campus includes academic buildings, laboratories, hostels, sports facilities, libraries and student activities.
